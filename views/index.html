Mentorship Programme

Connecting Experience
with Ambition

Our structured mentorship programme bridges the gap between experienced IUIU graduates and current students navigating their careers. Powered by AlumnConnect's intelligent matching system.

1
Create Your Profile
Alumni register their industry, skills, years of experience, and availability. Students list their career goals and academic focus.
2
Smart Matching
AlumnConnect's AI chatbot analyzes profiles and suggests the most compatible mentor-mentee pairings based on mutual criteria.
3
Connect & Grow
Send a connection request through the portal. Once accepted, begin your mentorship journey with structured guidance materials.
4
Track Progress
Both parties rate their sessions and report milestones. The alumni office monitors and supports all active mentorship relationships.
